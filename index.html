3D scan (EinScan etc.)

The process: Load scan → Measure whole car — one guided pass that aligns off the four lower-arm pivots, then walks you through every chassis and spindle point (~25 clicks). Nothing assembles until the last click, so half-measured states can't fail on you. On stands at full droop with wheels off is fine. S skips a step · H hides the model · Esc cancels (undoes everything).
