Parts & chassis

▸ edit
The car is built from parts: chassis pickups you measure ONCE, plus part cards (arms, spindle, tie rods) you read off the bench. Alignment is an output — there is no camber input anywhere.

x = fore/aft from front-axle center (+ = forward)
out = distance out from car centerline (always +)
z = height off the ground at reference ride
